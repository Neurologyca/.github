# Getting help

The public repositories of Neurologyca are read-only mirrors of the published packages. Issues and pull requests are not monitored there. Use the channels below instead.

## Start with the documentation

- [Quickstart](https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/start/quickstart/): from your API key to your first indicators.
- [Errors, warnings and retries](https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/sdk/errors/): every error of the SDK and what to do about it.
- [Known pitfalls](https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/resources/pitfalls/): integration traps we have measured, with their cause and fix.
- [SDK changelog](https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/resources/changelog/): breaking changes and the compatibility policy.

## Contact support

Email [support@neurologyca.com](mailto:support@neurologyca.com) and include:

- the package and version (`npm ls @neurologyca/capture @neurologyca/capture-server @neurologyca/kodec`);
- the browser and operating system, the integration mode (on-device or deferred) and the environment (test or production);
- the error code or warning, the `captureId` if there is one, and what you expected to happen;
- your DevLab organization. **Never send your API key or a token.**

## Other topics

- Security vulnerabilities: follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md). Do not email them to support.
- Legal, privacy and data-protection enquiries: [info@neurologyca.com](mailto:info@neurologyca.com)
- Licensing and commercial agreements: [legal documents](https://devlab.neurologyca.com/legal/) and [pricing](https://devlab.neurologyca.com/docs/access/pricing/)
